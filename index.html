How EduBridge Works

No accounts, no spam, no paywalls. A clean, privacy-first workflow designed around students.

1

Discover Opportunities

Browse clear, organized listings for college students in India. Search and filter by category, degree, eligibility requirements, or stipend without distracting popups.

2

Save What Interests You

Click the bookmark icon on any opportunity. Everything is stored directly in your browser's localStorage, remaining accessible whenever you return on the same device.

3

Track and Apply

Open your personal Dashboard to view saved deadlines, review requirements, and access direct application destinations when you are ready to apply.
