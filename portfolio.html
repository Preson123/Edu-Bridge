Architecture & Public Publishing Details
EduBridge runs on GitHub Pages static hosting with Firebase Authentication.
  • When you click Publish, your public project cards and bio are compiled into a shareable link that external visitors can open directly without signing in.
  • Private resume drafts, account phone numbers, and unverified details are strictly excluded from public URLs.
  • For automated multi-device cloud synchronization without URL parameters, connecting Cloud Firestore with owner-write and public-read rules provides the standard persistent backend.
